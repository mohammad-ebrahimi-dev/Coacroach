# Phase 1 source research

Research date: 25 November 2026. This is a source and permissions assessment, not a market study; no user content or datasets were collected. See [the source registry](../docs/source-registry.md) for evidence, access and restrictions. Sources and terms may change and must be checked again before use.

## Best three to investigate next

1. **World Bank WDI, Iran female labor-force participation indicator** ? the specific Iran series is visible and labeled CC BY 4.0, with documented structured API and downloads. This is the strongest first route for an approved, aggregate baseline. It does not explain individual experiences or validate an opportunity.
2. **Iran Open Data** ? Iran-focused primary-source-derived structured material could help identify relevant local datasets. Its terms require express written authorization for automated API access and do not clearly establish this project's commercial usage; seek written clarification, do not automate or collect under an assumption.
3. **The Tehran self-care survey article** ? an identifiable Iran-specific peer-reviewed survey with an explicit CC BY 4.0 article license. Investigate the study's methods and whether safe, aggregate-only reuse is appropriate. Publisher/API collection terms and the handling of sensitive health material remain separate questions; no respondent-level data or direct quotes are approved.

## Findings and limitations

The World Bank indicator is approved only for the documented aggregate series and published API/download route, subject to attribution and terms. An article is scoped to manual use of its published non-identifying aggregate findings, not automated scraping, sensitive personal material or participant data. Both are contextual evidence rather than direct proof of unmet needs.

The GitHub review dataset claims a CC BY license, but publisher authority to license underlying third-party reviews and its stated research-only intent versus commercial research need clarification. Google Play's official API applies to authorized app owners, not arbitrary competing apps. Iran Open Data places specific limits on automation. Stack Exchange permits some CC BY-SA reuse but its bot policy and API terms constrain routes and purposes. NiniSite is excluded under this project's instruction despite earlier site-owner research permission in conversation: that earlier authorization has not been shown to cover this project or commercial reuse.

Verified facts are linked at the individual source entries. **Unverified:** actual rights chain of the review dataset, commercial scope of IOD, permission for arbitrary app and forum extraction, and any source's relevance to a specific business. **Assumptions:** the listed domains may contain useful evidence; no claim of profitability follows. **Legal review:** jurisdiction, sensitive-data legal basis, commercial and downstream licensing, third-party rights, attribution and anonymization remain unresolved where applicable. Six domains are research directions, not findings; beauty and relationships in particular lack an approved source of direct personal experiences at this stage.

Next: confirm source-specific rights and safe access with the relevant administrators, identify additional primary Iranian datasets and openly licensed research, and review legal/privacy questions before collecting. Proceed to architecture only after the user authorizes Phase 2.
