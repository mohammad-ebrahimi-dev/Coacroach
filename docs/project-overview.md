# Iran market problem discovery

Phase 1 source research ? 25 November 2026. The aim is to identify evidence-backed unmet needs in Iran without assuming that any topic is profitable. Initial domains: motherhood and childcare; health and wellness; beauty and personal care; relationships and family; women's employment and income; daily life, shopping, and household services. Sources must be independent of any single site.

This phase assesses potential sources and records evidence in [the registry](source-registry.md). No datasets were collected and no crawler or analysis was implemented. Later phases require source-specific approval, documented permissions and a privacy assessment. Aggregate statistics can provide context but do not establish individual complaints, market demand or willingness to pay. NiniSite is excluded from collection until the explicit permission and intended usage scope are verified for this project.
