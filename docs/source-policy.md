# Source admission policy

A source is a specific dataset, version, channel and purpose, not blanket permission for a website. **Approved** means evidence supports the exact limited access, data and use described in its registry entry; it does not authorize broader scraping, sensitive-content ingestion, or other datasets. **Requires Review** means a necessary right, access condition, privacy safeguard or intended use remains unresolved; do not collect. **Rejected** means the proposed route or scope is excluded or conflicts with an identified restriction. Reassess when terms or purpose change.

Before admitting any source document its owner, URL, access method, license and terms links, date/version, permitted automation, commercial use and attribution obligations; check third-party rights, robots rules where applicable, quotas, restrictions, provenance and retention. An open license does not by itself grant access through every endpoint, ownership of third-party content, or a privacy-law basis. No disguised clients, control bypass, or unauthorized scraping. Stop on access denial, CAPTCHA and rate limits. Conservative sequential requests must comply with source-specific rules.

Do not collect usernames, profile links, emails, phone numbers or unnecessary identifiers. Free text and seemingly anonymous records can reveal health, family or other sensitive information; exclude such content until a valid legal basis and appropriate safeguards are established. Avoid preserving identifiers in URLs and logs. Normalization and redaction are not guaranteed de-identification. Plan access restrictions, deletion, retention and incident handling before collection.

Professional review is needed for applicable Iranian and other jurisdictional privacy and copyright law, lawful basis for sensitive information, validity of third-party sublicensing, commercial research scope, and obligations that could conflict with data minimization. These are questions, not legal conclusions. Do not infer user consent or permission from a public page, a robots allow rule, or a repository license alone.
